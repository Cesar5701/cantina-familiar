## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the authority, patina, and deliberate restraint of a historic spirit ledger crossed with high-end editorial wine criticism. Designed for collectors, discerning hosts, and hospitality professionals auditing fine agaves, single malts, and cellar-grade vintages across purveyors, the interface evokes the quiet gravitas of an archival tasting salon.

The design movement combines **Editorial Traditionalism** with **Modern Information Density**:
- **Tactile Parchment & Raw Sienna Tone:** Clean paper-like backgrounds that suggest physical estate folios and bonded distillery registers without faux-distress.
- **Archival Precision:** Dense, impeccably aligned comparisons, pricing metrics, and proof calculations rendered with surgical clarity.
- **Distillery Badging:** Vintage tax-stamp and batch-seal motifs used sparingly to signify audited authenticity, retail verification, and batch rarity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural agaves, toasted white oak, charred barrel staves, and unbleached ledger paper. 

- **Primary (`#8C5332` - Terracotta Stave):** Used for authoritative interactive signals, verified status marks, and leading brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#C8A27A` - Milled Brass):** Highlights price advantage differentials, cellar recommendations, and secondary interactive borders.
- **Tertiary (`#2B1E16` - Roasted Agave / Char):** Dominates high-emphasis typography, column headers, and structural rules.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7` - Alabaster Paper & `#F5EFEB` - Warm Almond):** Creates an organic, glare-free backdrop that feels bound and tactile.
- **Deep Obsidian (`#1C130D`):** Reserved for maximal-contrast data readouts, monospaced comparison rows, and archival stamp prints.
- **Neutral Accent Border (`#EADCC9` - Warm Parchment):** Defines structural container seams and tabular dividers without harsh digital division.

## Typography

The typographic tension relies on three distinct voices:
1. **Playfair Display (Editorial/Humanist Serif):** Infuses centuries-old viticultural and distillation pedigree into mastheads, spirit designations, appellations, and estate titles.
2. **Source Sans 3 (Functional Humanist Sans):** Delivers clean readability across analytical tasting reviews, organoleptic profiles, provenance notes, and producer biographies.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Audited Data & Metric Tabular):** Anchors price-per-liter calculations, ABV/proof readings, bottle serial notations, NOM registrations, and comparative retailer matrices. Set with tabular lining numbers to guarantee vertical column integrity across price matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric multi-column ledger structure referencing estate cellaring manifests and broadsheet auction catalogs.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive grid with `2rem` gutters and `3.5rem` outer margins. Accommodates 6-column retailer matrices with persistent sticky spec sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` canvas padding; retailer pricing switches to horizontal side-by-side comparative cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins; dense audit matrices transform into accordion ledgers with sticky pricing-per-liter metric footers.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows strict structural cadences (`space-sm` for metric badges, `space-md` for tabular rows, `space-xl` for estate-level sectioning) to preserve editorial white space.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids digital drop shadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **delicate linear boundaries**:

- **Ground Level:** `#FDFBF7` (Alabaster Paper) hosts broadsheet text, editorial essays, and primary catalog streams.
- **Elevated Ledger Panels:** `#F5EFEB` (Warm Almond) with crisp 1px borders of `#EADCC9` (Warm Parchment) to set apart comparative data tables.
- **Archival Overlay & Floating Dock:** `#2B1E16` (Deep Char) with subtle 1px inner brass illumination (`rgba(200, 162, 122, 0.25)`), creating an authentic stamp or dark wooden tasting flight tray.
- **Highlight Focus:** Active comparison targets receive an inset brass hairline ring (`1px solid #C8A27A`) instead of blurry diffused drop shadows.

## Shapes

The interface embraces crisp, archival edges. A conservative radius (`0.25rem` base, `0.5rem` large) honors cut-paper labels, index cards, and brass tax plaques:

- **Audit Badges & Tags:** Straight square corners (`0px`) or subtle bevels evoking revenue stamps and wax seal geometry.
- **Comparison Panels & Cellar Containers:** `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`), delivering just enough softness to sit naturally on screen without feeling modern-bubble or amorphous.
- **Control Inputs & Segmented Toggles:** `0.25rem` corner radius, creating the disciplined precision of hand-ruled ledger books.

## Components

### Buttons & Selection Triggers
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Audit Best Price", "Reserve Bottle"):** Solid `#8C5332` background, `#FDFBF7` monospace label text, `0.25rem` radius, subtle 1px border of `#2B1E16`. Active state shifts to `#2B1E16`.
- **Secondary / Cellar Compare:** Parchment surface (`#F5EFEB`) framed by a 1px `#C8A27A` brass border and `#2B1E16` text. Hover shifts background to `#EADCC9`.

### Retail Comparison Matrices & Price Tables
- Structured table rows built on `#FDFBF7` alternating with `#F5EFEB`.
- Retail chains (Total Wine, BevMo, Spec's, etc.) labeled in `label-sm` with a circular 6px inventory availability dot (Forest Green for In Stock, Terracotta for Low Stock).
- Unit metrics and price-per-liter (`$/L`) rendered strictly in `JetBrains Mono` bold for instant vertical scannability.
- Optimal market price accented with a fine `#C8A27A` badge border.

### Archival Stamp Badges & NOM / Proof Marks
- Bordered by a delicate 1px dashed or dual-stroke outline in `#8C5332` or `#C8A27A`.
- Houses verification metadata: "NOM 1146", "SINGLE CASK", "ESTATE BOTTLED", and batch numbers in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Inputs & Filter Strips
- Inset parchment boxes with `1px solid #EADCC9` borders, shifting to `1px solid #8C5332` upon focus.
- Monospaced numeric range sliders for Proof/ABV and Price boundaries, accompanied by live vintage-year indicators.

### Tasting & Cellar Cards
- Bound by 1px parchment borders (`#EADCC9`) on `#F5EFEB` backgrounds.
- Header featuring `headline-md` serif titles paired with right-aligned tabular price tags.
- Organoleptic profiles detailed via horizontal rating bars textured in terracotta (`#8C5332`) against sand tracks.
